# Mapping guidelines

What the Beat Saber mapping community (bsmg.wiki) says a good map does, and how beat-weaver applies
each point. Sources: [Basic](https://bsmg.wiki/mapping/basic-mapping.html),
[Intermediate](https://bsmg.wiki/mapping/intermediate-mapping.html),
[Downmapping](https://bsmg.wiki/mapping/downmapping.html),
[Extended](https://bsmg.wiki/mapping/extended-mapping.html) and the
[Glossary](https://bsmg.wiki/mapping/glossary.html). Numbers marked *favourites* were measured over
the 535 hand-picked reference maps used for the evaluator bands.

Three columns matter for each guideline: what the wiki says, where beat-weaver enforces it
(generation flag, evaluator metric, validator check), and what is still open. The open items are
collected in the [backlog](#backlog) at the end.

## 1. Representation: the map follows the music

| Guideline | Wiki | beat-weaver |
|---|---|---|
| Map real sounds. Most songs need only 1/1, 1/2, 1/4 precision; 1/8 and finer is usually overmapping. | Basic: Timing | Onset head on a 1/16 grid, `--min-gap`. `--precision-prior` scales each frame's onset probability by `inference.PRECISION_WEIGHT` (1/4 positions 1.0, 1/8 0.6, 1/16 0.4) before ranking, budget unchanged; mapper `SHAPING["precision_prior"]` 0.0 (off): A/B on 4 songs x 3 difficulties x 2 seeds cost 0.8 composite points (88.5 -> 87.7), and the new evaluator metric `fine` (share of notes at 1/8 beat or finer, offset-corrected, triplets on-grid; favourites p90 Easy 0.02, Normal 0.20, Hard 0.29, Expert 0.28, Expert+ 0.51) shows why: generated maps already sit at 0.006-0.010 without the prior, so it has nothing to fix. |
| Undermapping is fine, overmapping is not. | Basic | Scheduler budget `--notes-per-second`, rests on quiet beats (`--beat-floor`). |
| Density follows the song's energy. | Intermediate: Rhythm choice | `--dynamics`; evaluator `energy_corr`, `onset_hit`. |
| Emphasis ladder: single, 2-slider, stack, 3-slider, window, tower. Big sound, big swing. If everything is emphasized nothing is. | Basic: Emphasis | `--emphasis FRAC` stacks the loudest isolated hits; `--sliders FRAC` 2-dot sliders on held sounds; at Expert+ the loudest quarter of the emphasised hits become towers or windows (`shaping.emphasize`). Doubles via `--doubles-bias`/`--max-doubles`. |
| Consistency: the same sound mapped the same way; similar sections mapped similarly, mirrored or varied within the motif. | Intermediate: Consistency, Structure | `--repeats SIM` finds repeated 4-bar blocks by beat-synchronous mel similarity and copies the first occurrence onto them, mirroring odd repeats (`shaping.find_repeats`, `repeat_sections`). |
| Lead-in and tail: no notes in silence. | Basic | `--lead-in`, music-end trim; evaluator `lead_in`. |

## 2. Flow and parity

| Guideline | Wiki | beat-weaver |
|---|---|---|
| Parity: forehand/backhand alternate per hand. A "double directional" (same swing twice in a row) is a reset. | Basic: Parity | `--reset-gap` masks same-way cuts within the gap; `--flow-penalty` prefers the cut that continues the saber's arc; body sim counts `resets`; validator flags double directionals within 1.5 beats above the favourites' p90 rate per difficulty. |
| Time scaling: resets are fine when the hand has had time (about 1.5 beats) to come back. | Intermediate: Spacing | `--flow-window` fades the flow penalty to 0 over the last beat of the window (default 1.5). |
| Rotation amounts: 180° and 135° are safe at any speed; 90° maybe; 45° is a reset. Larger changes need more time. | Basic: Parity | Arc penalty is cosine-shaped so 180° is cheapest and 45° worst. |
| Momentum: a wrist turning one way wants to keep turning, up to a point; then it must unwind. | Intermediate: Momentum and Tension | `--momentum-bias` rewards cuts that keep the rotation sense, flipping to a penalty after 3 same-sense turns. |
| Swing curvature: positive (natural arc) fine to 135°; negative beyond 45° is harsh. | Intermediate: Rotations | `inference.CURVATURE` adds to the arc penalty when the new cut turns back against the sense of last-cut → travel and the cuts are more than 45° apart. Implemented but **off (0.0)**: at 0.3 it cost ~1.5 composite points over 48 maps and pushed `updown` out of band in 22 of 48 Expert/Expert+ maps. |
| Avoid awkward same-colour curves, triangles (three cuts rotating one way), Shrado angles, staircase hitbox abuse, Loloppe notes. | Basic, Glossary | Momentum tension cap covers triangles. Evaluator `staircase` (same-colour same-time diagonal pair cut across the diagonal, per hit). Evaluator `shrado` and `loloppe` (backlog 10). |

## 3. Spatial patterns

| Guideline | Wiki | beat-weaver |
|---|---|---|
| Hands move. Streams sweep sideways (piano stream: alternating colour and direction across the grid), jumps change lane or row. A hand parked in one cell is "inline". | Glossary: Piano Stream, Jump, Inline | `--drift-bias` rewards landing one lane over in the hand's current drift direction, reversing at the edges. Evaluator `lateral` (mean lanes moved per same-hand swing, favourites Expert 0.55–1.20), `inline` (share that stays put, p90 0.35), `zigzag3` (monotonic 3-lane runs per 100 pairs, Expert 0.7–9.4). |
| Vision blocks: nothing on the two centre middle cells that hides the next note. | Basic: Vision | `--vision-penalty`, `--vision-max-run`; evaluator `vision`, `vision_run3`. |
| Crossovers: a crossed hand keeps cutting the way it reached (right hand in columns 0-1 cuts left-ish); cutting back toward its own side is near unhittable. Easy and Normal: none. | Basic, Downmapping | Cut-back crossover tokens masked and dropped by the final filter (`_OUTWARD`, reversed 2026-10-20: 300 real Expert maps cut L/UL/DL 80% and R/UR/DR 1% for a right note in column 0); `far_cross` metric; downmapper drops crossovers for Easy/Normal. |
| Handclaps, arm tangles, controller clash: hands converging on the same cell or crossing with opposite vertical cuts. | Basic | Shared cell masked; converging pairs masked (handclaps); validator "crossed hands". Arm tangle: decoder mask in `_converging_right_tokens` (crossed LEFT with a vertical cut blocks opposite-vertical RIGHT cuts in the left half), evaluator `tangle`. |
| Cut-through: a cut whose blade path runs over the other colour's note (red above blue, both cutting up) hits the wrong note first. Favourites Expert/Expert+: 0.3% of doubles. | Basic: Hitbox | Decoder mask (`_converging_right_tokens`: 1–2 cells along either cut), evaluator `converging`, `shaping._resolve_collisions` after emphasis/repeats/parity repair (straighten, reverse, trim the stack, drop), validator "cut-through". |
| Top row sparingly (shoulder strain), especially at Easy/Normal. Down-cuts on the top row and inward doubles on the edges are hard for short players. Forehand comfortable in the inner lanes, backhand in the outer. | Basic: Difficulty, Intermediate: Handedness | `top_row` metric; the ladder keeps Easy/Normal off the top row. `--hand-bias` adds a zero-mean comfort table to each hand's logits (`inference._hand_comfort`): forehand inner, backhand outer, no down-cut on the top row or up-cut on the bottom, no outward horizontal on the edge. |
| Hitbox: no two notes of one colour that one swipe cannot cover. | Basic | Validator "wide doubles"; evaluator `converging`. |

## 4. Difficulty spread (downmapping)

| Guideline | Wiki | beat-weaver |
|---|---|---|
| Lower difficulties are the top difficulty distilled: keep the key moments, drop about 20% of the notes per step. | Downmapping | `--downmap Easy Normal` derives lower difficulties from the generated one (`shaping.downmap`): hits removed whole (parity untouched), weakest beats first (off-grid, quiet, non-downbeat), never two neighbours in a row inside a beat so streams become half-time streams. The mapper generates Hard and downmaps Easy and Normal from it; Expert and Expert+ are generated at their own density. |
| Easy and Normal: no crossovers, few top-row notes, dots acceptable, 2–3 (Easy) or 4–5 (Normal) unique patterns per half-jump interval. | Downmapping | Pattern ladder below (`shaping.TIERS`, `gate_patterns`), applied to every exported difficulty. `max_cells` caps distinct (hand, cell) placements per 2 bars (Easy 3, Normal 5); extras move to the hand's commonest cell. |
| Each difficulty is denser than the one below. | Basic: Difficulty | Mapper `LADDER_RATIO` 1.10; validator ladder check. |
| Reference densities (notes/s): Easy ~0.8, Normal ~1.1, Hard ~1.9, Expert ~2.4 (official). | Basic | Favourites bands per difficulty in `beat_sim/rhythm.py` (`nps`). |

### Pattern ladder

Each difficulty may use the patterns of its row and every row above it. `shaping.gate_patterns` runs on every
exported difficulty (generated or downmapped) and converts anything above the tier into the nearest allowed
shape rather than dropping it: diagonals lose their sideways part, horizontals become alternating up/down
cuts, a crossed-over hand moves back to its own side, top-row notes drop a row, stacks and sliders collapse
to one note, doubles thin to the cap and lane jumps are shortened. Hits closer than the minimum gap are
dropped. Parity is repaired afterwards.

| Difficulty | Unlocks | Cuts | Hand side | Rows | Doubles | Lane jump | Min gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | singles, up/down, dots | up, down, dot | own half only | bottom two | ≤ 18% | 1 | 1 beat |
| Normal | horizontals, 2-lane moves | + left, right | own half only | bottom two | ≤ 20% | 2 | 1/2 beat |
| Hard | diagonals, inward crossovers, top row, stacks | all | free | all | free | 3 | none |
| Expert | sliders, piano streams (drift bias) | all | free | all | free | 3 | none |
| Expert+ | towers (3 in an outer column) and windows (one-cell gap) on the loudest quarter of emphasised hits | all | free | all | free | 3 | none |

Outward crossovers are masked at generation for every difficulty. Downmapping aims at the absolute density in
`shaping.TARGET_NPS`, not just a ratio of the source. Easy's target (1.2/s) sits near the bottom of the favourites
band on purpose: the favourites are an Expert+ player's picks and their Easy runs well above official Easy
(~0.8/s). The evaluator's Easy `updown` band is open at the top because the tier makes every cut vertical.

## 5. Jump settings

| Guideline | Wiki | beat-weaver |
|---|---|---|
| Set reaction time, not NJS. Expert+ 500–600 ms, Hard 600–750 ms, Easy/Normal 750–1200 ms. NJS 16 is the Expert+ baseline; above 25 never. Keep NJS low and use the offset to fix reaction time. | Intermediate: Jump Settings | `exporter.jump_settings` computes `_noteJumpStartBeatOffset` from BPM and NJS to pin reaction time per difficulty (Easy 1200, Normal 1050, Hard 900, Expert 750, Expert+ 600 ms, matching favourites' medians). NJS: Easy 10, Normal 11, Hard 12, Expert 15, Expert+ 18. Validator flags reaction times outside 400–1500 ms. |

## 6. Playability and comfort

| Guideline | Wiki | beat-weaver |
|---|---|---|
| Effective BPM: 100–150 natural, 150–200 tiring, 200+ speed maps. Same-hand sequences feel much faster than alternating ones. | Intermediate: Swing Speed | Evaluator `ebpm` (p90 of 60 / per-hand swing gap, favourites p90: Easy 142, Normal 239, Hard 280, Expert 308, Expert+ 384); `--max-ebpm` masks a hand that swung too recently so the other hand takes the slot (mapper presets use the favourites p90); validator flags eBPM above the cap with 10% headroom. |
| Gallops (two singles then a double) are hard and add unintended emphasis. | Glossary | `--max-ebpm` stops the fast ones. Evaluator `gallop` (single, single, double within half a beat of each other, per 100 hits). |
| Leaning, swaying, crouching: regular, predictable, started on forehand swings. | Intermediate: Full Body Motion | `--walls` crouch walls in long rests only; no dodge walls or leaning: backlog 12. |
| Bombs: represent a sound; keep 1/4 beat clear before and after notes; resets at 2/3 of the gap. | Intermediate: Bombs | `--bombs` resets at 2/3 of the gap, 1/4 beat clear; no cosmetic bombs: backlog 12. |
| Arcs and chains: sparingly, for select swings. Walls: standard full-height and crouch only, never 3-wide, never the whole grid. | Extended, Basic | Crouch walls via `--walls`; arcs and chains not generated (v3 only): backlog 12. |
| Playtest with fresh players; mapper blindness is real. | Intermediate: Readability | `beat_sim` body simulation stands in. Player feedback system: CLAUDE.md open question. |
| Lighting: lights follow the notes and the song's energy; dark in rests. | Basic Lighting | Rules-based lightshow on every export (`lighting.light_events`, `--no-lights` to skip): back lasers and the hand's rotating laser flash in the note colour, ring lights fade per bar, centre lights on the loudest third of bars, ring spin every 8 bars, zoom on loudness jumps, laser speed from density, lights out in rests over 2 beats and at the end. |

## Play-tested Expert recipe

These are the Expert and Expert+ flags from ten rounds of in-game play testing (fix1–fix10, 2026-10). The tests used 4 songs of different styles, called songs A–D in the backlog below. The flags live in `beat_sim/mapper.py` as `RECIPE`, which the mapper and `scripts/batch_generate.py` both use. It needs the run 5 model, which is `models/current` since 2026-10-21 (`beat-weaver-extras-v5`, from `output/extras7/checkpoints/best`, epoch 9, val 2.185). Older models predate the stack tokens, so they rarely stack whatever the flags say.

| Flag | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `--notes-per-second` | 3.6–4.2 | 4.2 for busy songs, 3.6 for the rest. The mapper's Expert sweep is 3.1, 3.7 and 4.2. |
| `--doubles-bias` / `--max-doubles` | 3.5 / 0.3 | Brings doubles up to the real median of about 15 per 100 notes, with a cap. The 59-song batch made doubles at 22% of hits, matching the favourites' 21%. |
| `--max-ebpm` | 350 | Above this, fast same-hand runs tire players. |
| `--crossover-penalty` | 2 | Real maps cross over about 20 times per 100 notes. A crossed hand cuts outward, and the cut-back mask always applies (see below). |
| `--reversal-penalty` | 4 | Avoids awkward reversals on consecutive notes. A down-right then up-left swing is fine for parity, so this can't stop inward cuts in the outer column. The mask does that. |
| `--raised-max-run` | 3 | Long runs in the upper row tired players quickly on two of the four songs (fix7). This caps a hand at 3 notes in a row in rows 1–2. |
| `--column-max-run` | 4 | Stops one hand staying in one column too long. |
| `--stacks --stack-rate 3.3 --stack-bias 4` | | Run 5 alone made 0–0.34 stacks per 100 notes, against 1.3 in real maps. Bias 4 gives about 3 per 100, the real p75. Bias 2 gave only 0.5–1.7, and two of the four songs fell out of band on `near_converging`. |
| `--streams` | 0.12 default | Use 0.18 for songs with long driving sections and 0.08 for songs that are already busy. The real median is about 10 stream notes per 100, the p75 about 18. |
| `--dynamics-range LO HI` | 0 2 default | Use `0.7 1.3` when a song has a few very busy sections and many sparse ones. One song passed on the average but felt uneven to play. |
| `--diagonal-bias` | 1.5 | Real maps have 31 diagonals per 100 notes. |
| `--parallel-bias` / `--parallel-streak` | 1.5 / 4 | Parallel doubles: both hands in one row, right hand 1–2 columns right, same cut, so they swing in sync. The favourites have 5.6 per 100 Expert notes and the 59-song batch had 4.0. The bias alone matched the rate but gave few runs. The streak bonus grows with each parallel hit in a row (up to 3), giving runs of 6+ and 63–109 in-sync notes per 100. Play-tested on Remedy (2026-10-24), liked over streak 0 and 2; composite unchanged. |
| `--hand-balance` | 10 | Keeps the two hands' note counts even. |
| `--bomb-bias 4 --bomb-gap-bias 8 --bomb-row-bias 3 --walls` | | Bombs go in gaps, off the saber's path. Walls go in rests. |
| `--flow-penalty 4 --vision-penalty 6 --stream-bias 1.0 --min-gap 0.1 --dynamics 1` | | Unchanged from the earlier presets. |

The mapper takes per-song knobs as `--extra`. Later flags win, so these override the recipe:

    python -m beat_sim.mapper "song.mp3" --difficulties Expert --extra "--notes-per-second 4.2 --streams 0.18 --dynamics-range 0.7 1.3"

The decoder enforces these rules whatever the flags say:
- A crossed hand never cuts back toward its own side.
- A hand in its own outer column never cuts inward. Real maps do it only 1–4% of the time, and players find it very awkward right after outward motion (fix9).
- A stack is only ever the next cell along the cut line.
- The map ends at the last hit at -20 dB or louder, not in a fading ring-out (fix9).

What doesn't work:
- `--emphasis`, which adds stacks after decoding. Use `--stack-bias` instead.
- Judging density by the song-wide average. It misses songs with a few very busy sections and many sparse ones; check the 8-beat window p90/p50 instead (real median 1.28, p90 1.63).

Real Expert maps (300 maps, per 100 notes, p25 / p50 / p75):

| Pattern | p25 | p50 | p75 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stacks | 0 | 1.3 | 3.3 |
| Stream notes | 2.8 | 9.9 | 18.5 |
| Doubles | 9.4 | 14.8 | 19.3 |
| Gallops | 1.4 | 3.0 | 4.7 |
| Wide doubles | 0.5 | 1.4 | 3.2 |
| Crossovers | 14.7 | 20.3 | 26.3 |
| Diagonals | 21.4 | 31.3 | 40.6 |
| Dots | 0.1 | 2.7 | 7.2 |

Windows, sliders and pianos are rare. Expert+ uses the same recipe at higher density (4.6 notes per second; Expert 3.6). On slow songs (80–96 BPM) the onset head finds few candidate hits, so Expert overshoots its target and Expert+ can't go higher: 4.6, 5.4 and 6.2 targets gave the same note count. `scripts/batch_generate.py` therefore re-generates Expert wherever it isn't 1.1× apart from both Hard and Expert+, aiming between them and bisecting over up to 4 rounds. On the 59-song batch that cleared 22 of 23 ladder warnings. Fix10's Expert scores were 88–95, and its Expert+ scores were 88–94.

## Lower difficulties (Easy, Normal, Hard)

Hard is generated once with `HARD_RECIPE` (`beat_sim/mapper.py`) and Easy and Normal are downmapped from it (`--downmap Easy Normal`). The mapper and `scripts/batch_generate.py` both do this. Generating Easy and Normal directly gave dense maps with poor parity.

Real maps (150 sampled per difficulty), per 100 notes, p25 / p50 / p75:

| Pattern | Easy | Normal | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notes per second | 1.3 / 1.6 / 2.1 | 1.9 / 2.3 / 3.0 | 2.7 / 3.2 / 4.2 |
| Doubles | 4.8 / 9.9 / 16.3 | 6.6 / 11.4 / 18.1 | 7.1 / 11.4 / 19.4 |
| Stacks | 0 / 0 / 0.8 | 0 / 0 / 2.7 | 0 / 0.7 / 3.1 |
| Stream notes | 0 / 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 / 0 | 0 / 2.4 / 10.3 |
| Gallops | 0 / 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 / 0.5 | 0 / 1.0 / 2.7 |
| Crossovers | 1.5 / 6.7 / 14.1 | 3.7 / 9.0 / 16.3 | 9.2 / 14.6 / 20.7 |
| Diagonals | 0 / 9.5 / 22.7 | 1.9 / 12.0 / 23.1 | 11.2 / 23.2 / 35.1 |

What changed, tested on the same four songs (mean score Easy / Normal / Hard):
- Old presets: 83 / 84 / 78. New: 80 / 85 / 85.
- Old Hard made 5 gallops per 100 notes (real median 1) and no stacks or crossover control. New Hard makes 1.7 gallops and 0.8 stacks, with crossovers and diagonals near the real medians.
- Hard's stack, stream and gallop flags are milder than Expert's, matching the table above. Stack bias 2 and rate 0.7, streams 0.05.
- `shaping.TARGET_NPS["Easy"]` went from 1.20 to 1.50. The downmapped Easy was landing at 1.0 notes per second, below the real band (1.09–1.83).
- `_simplify_cut` lets Normal keep a sideways cut after a vertical one. Normal was 98% up/down before, and the band is 57–83%.

Parallel doubles (2026-10-24). The 59-song batch made doubles at only 6.5% of Hard hits, against 24% in the favourites, and 1.6 parallel doubles per 100 notes against 8.8. `HARD_RECIPE` now has `--doubles-bias 2.0 --parallel-bias 2 --parallel-streak 4`, the same in-sync streaks as Expert (see the Expert table). The extra doubles add notes, so Hard density went from 3.1 to 2.8 notes per second (`DENSITY_SCALE["Hard"]` 1.035, the batch preset 2.8). On Remedy, 2 seeds:

| Hard | Doubles / hits | In-sync notes per 100 | Output nps | Composite |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Old recipe, 3.6 nps | 12.5% | — | — | 84.0 |
| New, 3.1 nps | 28% | 70–76 | 4.04–4.13 (band top 4.09) | 83–88 |
| New, 2.8 nps | 29% | 47–53 | 3.67 | 94–94.5 |

Known gaps:
- Easy and Normal still have no diagonals and no crossovers. The tier rules forbid them (`shaping.TIERS`), although a quarter of real Easy and Normal maps have some. That is a design choice to revisit, not a bug.
- Hard still often has too many notes in the top row, and sometimes `shrado`.

## The gap metrics were broken until 2026-10-22

`rhythm.stats` reused the name `g` for the `fine` timing check, overwriting the note-gap array. Every map's `gap_le_half`, `gap_1` and `gap_ge2` read about 0.55, 0.13 and 0. That made Easy and Normal look out of band on gaps. The apparent problem with their timing grid wasn't real. Scores from before the fix are lower than they should be for Easy and Normal, and slightly different elsewhere. The test `test_gap_stats_are_not_overwritten_by_the_fine_check` guards it.

## Where each rule lives

- Generation flags: `beat-weaver generate --help`; Expert/Expert+ recipe in `beat_sim/mapper.py` (`RECIPE`), lower difficulties in `PRESET`, `SHAPING`.
- Post-decode shaping: `beat_weaver/model/shaping.py` (downmap, repeats, emphasis).
- Evaluator metrics and bands: `beat_sim/rhythm.py`; recalibrate with `python -m beat_sim.calibrate_bands <favourites folder>`.
- Jump settings: `beat_weaver/model/exporter.py`.
- Validator: `beat-weaver validate <map folder>` (`beat_weaver/model/map_check.py`).
- Lighting: `beat_weaver/model/lighting.py`.

## Backlog

Numbering continues from the review that produced this document. Items 1–7, 9, 11, 14, 15 and 16 shipped on 2026-10-12; 8, 17 and most of 10 on 2026-10-13.

7. ~~Forehand/backhand placement biases~~ shipped: `--hand-bias` (`inference._hand_comfort`), mapper `SHAPING["hand_bias"]` 0.5.
8. ~~Rhythm precision prior~~ shipped but off by default in the mapper: `--precision-prior` / `inference.PRECISION_WEIGHT`. Evaluator `fine` shows generated maps are already well inside the favourites band (0.6-1.0% fine notes) without it, so it stays off. Vocal snapping shipped: `--vocal-snap LEAD` (`shaping.vocal_signal` + `shaping.vocal_snap`): librosa HPSS splits harmonic from percussive, a hit in a stretch where the smoothed harmonic/percussive onset ratio is >= LEAD moves onto the nearest harmonic onset peak (<= 1/4 beat, re-quantised to the nearer of the 1/8 and 1/12 grids). Off by default, un-A/B'd.
9. ~~Effective BPM cap~~ shipped: evaluator `ebpm`, `--max-ebpm` in generation (mapper presets per difficulty), validator check.
10. **Named bad patterns as explicit checks.** Shipped: evaluator `tangle`, `gallop`, `inline4` (4+ same-hand swings parked on one cell, per 100 pairs), `staircase`, plus the arm-tangle decoder mask. Handclaps are the existing `converging` metric/mask; triangles the momentum cap. The four new metrics are banded on the 535 favourites (weight 1 each): `tangle` and `staircase` have p90 of about 0, so any occurrence is out of band; `gallop` p90 0.96 (Easy) to 9.4 (Expert+) per 100 hits; `inline4` p90 about 3.4 per 100 pairs. Shipped 2026-10-13 from the glossary wording: `shrado` (per 100 same-hand pairs: outward diagonal-down in lane 0 or 3, then an up-cut 2+ lanes inward within a beat) and `loloppe` (per hit: same-colour, same-time, same-cut pair in orthogonally adjacent cells with the cut running across the join; a stack along the cut is clean). Bands (0, 0) by definition, weight 1; rerun `beat_sim/calibrate_bands.py` to confirm on the favourites. (Basic, Glossary)
11. ~~Validator additions~~ shipped: reaction-time range, max eBPM, double directionals within 1.5 beats. The density ladder already covered all five difficulties. Inline rate stays an evaluator metric only.
12. **Bombs, walls, arcs, chains.** (Songs A–D are the four play-test songs; "the reference song" is the first one used for scoring.) Bomb resets at 2/3 of a gap for long rests; cosmetic bombs clear of saber paths; standard dodge and crouch walls with time to return; arcs on select sustained notes. Bombs and walls shipped as export-time rules, off by default: `--bombs` (same-hand rest >= 2 beats returning on the same up/down cut gets a bomb at 2/3 of the gap in the saber's resting half, >= 1/4 beat from any note) and `--walls` (one full-width crouch wall per rest >= 4 beats, 1 beat in from each side) via `shaping.bombs_and_walls` and `export_notes(extras=)`. Model support (2026-10-13): the tokenizer has 12 BOMB tokens (one cell per 1/16 position) and 128 WALL tokens (x, width, full/crouch, duration bucket 0.5/1/2/4 beats) after the 355 note tokens, vocab 495. Grammar: RIGHT -> optional BOMB -> optional WALL -> next position. Fine-tune `configs/medium_conformer_extras.json` warm-starts from the placement model (`--init-from` grows the embedding and output rows); maps with more than 500 walls are dropped as modcharts. Model-made bombs and walls go on the generated difficulty, rule-made ones (`--bombs`, `--walls`) on all. Model walls and bombs pass `shaping.limit_extras` before export: walls under 1 beat, full-height walls 3+ lanes wide and walls covering a note are dropped, the rest capped at `--max-walls-per-bar` (0.5, longest first, wall starts kept 4 / cap beats apart so they cannot bunch in one section); bombs within 1/4 beat of a note in their column are dropped. On the reference song's Expert with the 20-epoch continuation (`output/extras2`, val_loss 2.2198 at epoch 16) this kept 20 of 698 model walls and 2 of 3 bombs; a global count cap had let 13 walls land in 5 bars. Played feedback on that map: right hand 69% in one bottom-centre cell (decode collapse; the share swings 0.27-0.69 between checkpoints at equal val loss, so use `--column-max-run 4`), bombs ~15x under training density (`--bomb-bias 2` gives 61 bombs, 6.7 per 100 notes vs 5 in training; 3 gives 19), notes under crouch walls (the cover rule now checks every row and the half beat after the wall). Second play (extras3): first half still easy (3.5 notes/s, 10-14% doubles, vs 4-5 and 25-37% in the second half).
